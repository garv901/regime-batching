Phase 1c · Pass E · Sequence

RELEASE_KV frees the pages at completion, before the client reads

MSTAR_KV_RELEASE_AT_COMPLETION, default off. Before: KV pages stay allocated until the conductor sends REMOVE_REQUEST, which happens after the client read the output. After: at completion the conductor sends RELEASE_KV, the worker frees the pages at once, and the later REMOVE_REQUEST frees nothing; the gate never re-admits a released request. Verdict: keep off by default.
