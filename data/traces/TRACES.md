## Bimodality notes
- Output lengths are heavy-tailed / mixed short-vs-long rather than cleanly two-peaked. Most heterogeneous (CV 2-3.3, p50 << p90 << p99): azure2024_code (p50 8, p99 271, max 5000), burstgpt_2/3 (p50 7-14, p99 480-910, max 5k-12k), burstgpt_1 (p50 36, p99 1505), mooncake_toolagent (p10 3, p50 30, p90 507: a short-tool-call mode plus a few-hundred-token mode).
- azure2024_conv and mooncake_conversation/azure2023_conv are more unimodal (hundreds of tokens, lower CV).
- azure2023_code: ~92% of outputs <=64 tokens (completion-like), no outputs >=1024.
- Almost no public production trace has >=4096-token outputs (<0.3%); only reasoning_openthoughts (R1-style generations, p50 ~6.5k, 69% >=4096) provides the long-output mode. Mixing a production short-output trace with reasoning_lengths.csv is the realistic way to get a short-vs-long bimodal mix.
- BurstGPT: rows with Response tokens == 0 (failures) were dropped.
- reasoning_lengths.csv: OpenThoughts-114k is a DeepSeek-R1 distillation set (first 20k streamed rows); lengths are the Qwen3-8B tokenization of conversations[0] (prompt) and [1] (response incl. thought); real Qwen3 thinking lengths may differ.
